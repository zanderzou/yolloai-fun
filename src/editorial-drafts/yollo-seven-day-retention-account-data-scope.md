**A seven-day statement should stay attached to the data category it describes.** Yollo's [app-published privacy policy](https://app.yollo.ai/privacy-policy.html) distinguishes uploaded AI-feature content from account information. Applying the same number to every record would expand the statement beyond its published scope.

The [website privacy page](https://www.yollo.ai/privacy) uses a broader active-account or service-need description. We did not audit either system's storage or establish which policy applies to a particular user's product context.

## Three categories to keep separate

| Category | Scope question |
| --- | --- |
| Account information | How long is the login-related record retained? |
| Uploaded content for AI features | Does the specific seven-day statement cover this item? |
| Other records or copies | What separate purpose or controller applies? |

The app policy says uploaded user content for AI features is temporarily stored and deleted within seven days, while account information is retained for the active account and relevant obligations. Those are provider statements, not results of a deletion experiment we performed.

## Do not silently rename every record an upload

A typed conversation, payment record and uploaded file are not interchangeable labels. If a policy passage names one category, do not assume it necessarily covers the others without an explicit definition or account-specific explanation.

For an illustrative case, a person reads the seven-day passage and concludes the login account itself expires a week later. The passage does not support that inference. Likewise, a retained account does not prove an uploaded file remains available for recovery.

## Keep website and app context visible

Record the service surface, policy URL and the date you read it. A product family can publish several documents, but the shared brand name alone does not resolve their exact scope or current applicability.

The website privacy page describes an account-deletion request route. A request acknowledgment is different from a completed-removal confirmation, and an inability to open an item does not constitute a full backend retention audit.

## Ask about the identified data

A useful clarification names the category and asks which policy, retention period and deletion outcome apply. Keep any response tied to the original account or item reference privately.

Do not upload new sensitive material to test a deadline, and do not assume retained operational information is a user-restorable archive. If local downloads or cloud copies exist under your control, their handling is a separate question.

The practical conclusion is limited: the published seven-day wording is category-specific. Accurate guidance keeps that limit visible rather than converting it into a blanket promise about all information associated with Yollo.
