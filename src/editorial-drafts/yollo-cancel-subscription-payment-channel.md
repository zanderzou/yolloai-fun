**The right Yollo cancellation route depends on the purchase record, not just the device in your hand.** The [website terms](https://www.yollo.ai/terms) identify the original payment partner, while the [app-published agreement](https://app.yollo.ai/user-agreement.html) describes contacting support. Preserve that difference rather than turning either page into a universal button-by-button promise.

We read these public pages on October 19, 2026. We did not inspect a paid account or verify which agreement applies to an individual purchase.

## Identify the billing manager

Keep the receipt, product, account context, purchase date and renewal information together. A mobile browser purchase is not automatically an app-store purchase. An installed app icon does not establish who took the payment either.

If the receipt names a payment platform, locate that platform's existing subscription record independently. If the purchase route is unclear, give official support the private receipt reference and ask who manages its renewal.

Do not subscribe again to make a management screen appear. A new purchase adds another billing record without explaining the old one.

## Keep the published routes in context

The website and app agreement use different cancellation wording. Possible explanations include product scope or an outdated document; public text alone does not decide which explanation applies.

The useful question is which conditions and manager govern the identified transaction. If support provides a route, preserve its response and the purchase reference so the instruction can be matched to the correct subscription.

## Confirm the resulting state

After an intended cancellation flow, keep the confirmation, resulting renewal state and paid access end date. Request receipt and completed cancellation are different events.

For an illustrative case, a ticket acknowledgment sent before renewal is not the same evidence as a manager showing renewal disabled. If no completion is confirmed, ask about the unresolved state while preserving the original request time.

## Separate the remaining questions

An account deletion concerns access and data. A refund concerns an existing payment. Cancellation concerns the next renewal. One confirmation should not silently be used as proof of all three.

The app agreement describes continuing access through the current paid period. If that is the applicable purchase context, remaining access after cancellation is not by itself proof that another renewal is active.

If a later charge looks inconsistent, compare the exact transaction with the confirmation and relevant receipt. Keep passwords and authentication codes out of the support record. The useful outcome is a confirmed billing state for the intended purchase, with any unresolved product-scope difference explicitly identified.
