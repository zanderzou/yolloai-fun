Yollo AI vs Nomi can involve two different expectations: remembering what happened in a story and maintaining a recognizable fictional relationship. These overlap, but they are not the same result. A reply can sound familiar while remembering the wrong event.

Public provider information was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This article provides an original non-explicit evaluation plan with adult fictional characters and no reported account tests.

## Create one event and one relationship preference

Imagine an adult fictional mapmaker and a visiting writer. They agreed to repair a torn chart before planning a journey. The mapmaker uses a friendly but restrained tone and asks for the writer's decisions rather than making them.

The torn chart is the plot item. The conversational tone and respect for decisions describe the relationship routine. Keep an external note of both so you can assess the next visit without relying on an overall impression.

| Dimension | Question to ask | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Plot recall | What task did we leave unfinished? | Correct chart repair, uncertainty or invention |
| Tone | Can we discuss the next step briefly? | Familiar style and manageable length |
| Player agency | Which route should I consider? | Options offered without a decided choice |
| Correction | The chart is torn, not missing | Response to the narrow correction |
| Continuation | What remains before departure? | Whether the corrected scene stays coherent |

## Compare the actual context route

Yollo publicly describes chat and media generation. Inspect the current character and history controls for the session you use. Do not interpret an image of the mapmaker as evidence that the conversation remembers the chart.

Nomi documents Backstory+ and a Mind Map view. Use available context tools according to their stated purpose, while recognizing that a displayed memory view is not a complete inventory of everything a service may retain or infer.

## Avoid scoring warmth as accuracy

On return, ask about the unfinished task before supplying its name. If the reply says the chart is missing, note that discrepancy even if the language is affectionate or reassuring. Then supply the correction and observe the continuation.

Conversely, a reply that correctly names the torn chart may still be too long, change the intended tone or decide the writer's route. These are useful observations about conversation fit, but they do not erase the successful plot recall.

## Choose the dimension you value most

For a structured writing project, stable event and world facts may be central. For an ongoing companion routine, tone and comfortable return may matter more. Define your priorities without claiming that either provider is limited to only one of those activities.

Keep the exercise non-sensitive and use original fiction. It can reveal the configured session's practical behavior; it cannot establish a health benefit or perfect long-term memory.
