Yollo AI vs Kindroid for story canon should focus on what happens after you revise a world fact. A polished first scene may not reveal whether the next conversation uses your updated premise. Separating the revision from visual changes makes the decision easier to assess.

Official information was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This is an original non-explicit writing exercise with adult fictional characters. It reports no generated samples or measured instruction-following scores.

## Start with a scene that has one clear rule

Imagine an adult curator preparing a fictional sky museum. The museum closes at sunset, and its roof terrace is available only during opening hours. The curator speaks plainly and asks the visitor before changing locations.

The planned revision changes closing time to one hour after sunset. Keep the terrace rule, the curator's role and the visitor's agency intact. Write the original and revised sentence in a small change record so you know exactly what you altered.

| Part of the scene | Status after the revision |
|---|---|
| Closing time | One hour after sunset |
| Terrace access | Still restricted to opening hours |
| Curator occupation | Unchanged |
| Dialogue style | Still plain and manageable |
| Visitor movement | Still an open player choice |

## Locate a supported persistent edit

Yollo's public site describes character and media tools. Inspect the current definition or creator controls available for your own character. If you use someone else's card, record whether you can edit its setup or only supply a correction in chat.

Kindroid documents personality controls. Use the relevant supported setup for the written rule and retain the previous text. Do not regenerate a whole personality solely to change a closing time; that would introduce additional variables into the exercise.

## Ask a question that depends on the new rule

After the revision, say that sunset has just passed and ask what options remain. Check whether the answer treats the museum as open for another hour and whether it leaves the visitor's next action undecided.

A reply that names the revised time but says the visitor already entered the terrace satisfies only part of the brief. Record the world-fact result and the player-agency result separately. Useful comparisons preserve neighboring requirements, not just the phrase you edited.

## Keep pictures out of the first check

If you later use an available permitted image feature, judge the picture against its visual brief. A nighttime museum image does not prove that the dialogue recognizes the revised opening hours. Likewise, a correct text reply does not show that every generated picture has the intended roof layout.

Track optional media charges and rejected attempts separately from character setup. Use original fictional assets and follow current source rules; there is no need to upload a real person's likeness for a canon exercise.
