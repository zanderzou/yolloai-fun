<p class="article-lede">Yollo AI combines character chat with image and video creation, so its risk surface is wider than a simple entertainment page. A useful safety review separates regional eligibility, conversation privacy, uploaded media, public characters, account security, and in-app spending.</p>

| Area | What to verify | Safer default |
|---|---|---|
| Region | Current Terms list prohibited jurisdictions | Do not access or bypass controls where prohibited |
| Chat | Conversations and interaction data may be processed | Use fictional names, places, and events |
| Creation | Characters or actions may be public | Check visibility before publishing |
| Media | Photos, clips, prompts, and outputs can be sensitive | Use non-sensitive fictional inputs first |
| Account | Email, password, device, and usage data may be handled | Use unique credentials and MFA if available |
| Spending | Apps can combine subscriptions and in-app purchases | Set a small cap and verify renewal terms |

## Check regional eligibility first

Yollo AI's current Terms list jurisdictions where the service is not intended to be available, including Mainland China and other restricted regions, and say users must not circumvent controls with VPNs or proxies. The current policy material is not perfectly consistent across every page, so read the version presented in your actual app or region.

If the service is prohibited where you are located, stop there. This independent guide does not provide bypass instructions.

## Treat roleplay chat as online service data

Fictional conversation can invite real disclosure. Yollo AI's privacy materials describe account information, interaction or usage data, and content connected with chat and character creation. Technical details such as IP address, device identifiers, app version, and approximate location may also be processed.

Use a fictional profile and scenario. Keep legal names, exact location, employer, financial information, health records, legal problems, passwords, and other people's secrets out of chat. A character that feels emotionally responsive is still software operating through an online service.

## Images and video add identity risk

The product promotes AI images, text-to-video, and image-to-video. Before uploading a personal photo or clip, verify the current rules for likeness consent, retention, deletion, model improvement, and sharing. Do not upload another person's intimate or identifying media without explicit permission and product-rule compliance.

Start with generated or otherwise non-sensitive fictional input. Inspect outputs for unintended identity changes, youth-coded appearance, anatomy errors, or background details before downloading or sharing.

## Public characters and creator visibility

A bot profile can reveal more than intended through its name, greeting, scenario, avatar, or backstory. Before publishing, confirm whether the character is private, unlisted, or searchable. Do not build a character from a real person's name, private history, face, or voice without permission.

Keep every romantic or adult-coded character unmistakably adult. Avoid school-age framing, ambiguous ages, or attempts to bypass content safeguards.

## Account deletion and retention

Current privacy material says users can request account deletion through settings or contact routes. Before investing heavily, locate the control and determine what happens to chats, characters, prompts, uploads, generated media, purchases, backups, and public posts.

Deleting an app from a phone does not necessarily delete the account. Keep a record of a formal deletion request without including private chat or media in the support message.

## Password and payment hygiene

Use a unique password that is not shared with email, cloud storage, or social media. Enable multifactor authentication if offered. Keep the app and operating system updated, and sign out on shared devices.

Before buying, verify the currency, subscription period, auto-renewal, included credits, one-time packs, generation cost, failed-action policy, cancellation route, and refund limits. Set a modest first-week cap.

## Bottom line

Yollo AI can be approached more safely only after confirming it is available in your jurisdiction. If eligible, use fictional details, non-sensitive visual inputs, private character settings, unique credentials, and a spending limit. Review live policies because app features and regional rules can change.

If you cannot find satisfactory answers about chat retention, media handling, deletion, or purchases, do not submit more sensitive content.
